## What Is Agile?

- **Definition**: Agile means *nimble*, *quick*, and *adaptive*.
- Agile is **not** a methodology—it's a **mindset** and **way of working**.
- You **don’t “do” Agile**; you **work in an agile way**.

---

## Agile Manifesto (2001)

### Values:

- **Individuals & interactions** over processes & tools  
- **Working software** over comprehensive documentation  
- **Customer collaboration** over contract negotiation  
- **Responding to change** over following a plan  

> While there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left more.

---

## Implications of Agile

- Focus on **delivering customer value**
- Accept and adapt to **change**
- Measure success through **working software**
- Collaborate closely with **customers**

---

## The Problem With “Agile”

- The term became a buzzword—over-commercialized with:
  - Consultants, conferences, certifications
- **Dave Thomas** (Manifesto author) suggests using the term **“Agility”** instead.

---

## What Good Agile Looks Like

- Understand your current state
- Take small steps toward goals
- Adjust as you learn
- Choose paths that support **future change**

---

## 🔧 Practical Agile Characteristics

- **Self-organizing teams**
- **Flexible plans and architectures**
- **Iterative development**
- **Empirical process control**
- **Value-based prioritization**
- **Time-boxing** (e.g., sprints)

---

## Agile in Action

### Requirements

- Use **Epics** and **User Stories**
- Prioritize using techniques like **Planning Poker**
- Represent users through **Personas**

### Sprints

- Deliver **shippable product** every Sprint
- Use **velocity** to forecast timelines
- Adapt requirements as understanding evolves

### Teams

- Self-managed, no traditional managers
- Encourage **servant leadership**
- Output is a **team effort**

### Work Tracking

- Tools: GitHub Projects, Trello, Jira, Post-Its
- Use **Kanban** to visualize and limit **WIP (Work In Progress)**

---

## Agile Mindset in Practice

- Early discovery of blockers is faster in agile teams
- Ability to **pivot** is crucial
- Avoid large upfront planning (like Gantt charts)

---

## Frameworks & Approaches

- **Scrum**: Widely used *framework* (often customized as “Scrum-But”)
- **Lean**: Emphasizes innovation and iterative feedback  
  *(Build → Measure → Learn)*
